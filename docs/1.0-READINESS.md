# Graph Auto Reader 1.0 readiness

## Decision

**FAIL.** Version `0.0.21` is an internal development checkpoint. Its build
number is on a public-release cadence, but the release is not ready and must
not be tagged or published. Required production models, final NCNN production
approval, private automatic supplied-example evidence, release artifacts, and
complete installer and portable clean-Windows workflow evidence are absent.
The exact OpenCV and PDFium component clean-machine gates now pass, but they do
not satisfy the remaining production workflow or distribution gates.

`PASS` below means direct repository, command, or runtime evidence satisfies the
named gate. Component-level tests do not satisfy an end-to-end or clean-machine
gate. `FAIL` means the gate failed, is blocked, or has not been directly proven.
