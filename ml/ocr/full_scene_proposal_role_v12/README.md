# OCR full-scene proposal and role V12

V12 is a new three-candidate defect class preregistered from aggregate, already-consumed V3 C# composition evidence. It may not read or reuse V3 fixture bytes, scene truths, Chandler, private images, article images, or any predecessor split.

P1 keeps the production adaptive-gray proposal grouping and the existing `[N,2,32,144]` input and `[N,10]` output contracts. The isolated repair is a new independent variable-layout scientific-graph renderer, deterministic round-robin coverage of structure-family hard negatives, and a geometry-gated visual encoder. It targets exact text-region counts, at least `0.90` role accuracy, at least `0.85` for every role, CPU ONNX parity at most `1e-5`, and the unchanged downstream recognition and marker gates.

The split was generated once after design preregistration. Its tracked manifests bind 480 training scenes, 120 validation scenes, 160 hidden-public scenes, exact proposal fingerprints, five hard-negative families, candidate configuration, and runner and evaluator source bundles.

P1 is consumed and failed closed. It found all 960 validation text regions with zero misses and classified all eight role families exactly, but one structural proposal remained at the best fixed threshold, so only 119 of 120 scenes were exact. CPU ONNX maximum absolute parity error was `2.09808349609375e-05`, above the fixed `1e-05` limit.

P2 is consumed. Its zero-optimizer `0.4` logit scale passed CPU ONNX parity at `4.291534423828125e-06` and retained all 960 regions and roles exactly, but the same one structural false proposal remained.

P3 is consumed and passed the visible selection gate. It reuses the exact P2 ONNX bytes with zero optimizer steps and adds only a source-geometry structural guard: reject a proposal when it contains exactly one connected component, its ink density is at most `0.30`, and its width-to-height ratio is at least `2.75`. The guard rejects 493 non-text training proposals and 118 non-text validation proposals while rejecting zero truth-matched proposals on either visible split. P3 executed all 120 validation scenes on CPU, preserved all 960 truth regions and all eight role families exactly, and removed the single accepted structural false region. It cannot use scene identity, truth, role, semantic content, graph position, Chandler, private data, or public fixture bytes. The hidden-public archive remains ignored and unopened. Exactly one checksum-bound public run is now authorized. Production approval and release eligibility remain false until the gate runs and all later composition, provenance, store, and packaging evidence passes.
