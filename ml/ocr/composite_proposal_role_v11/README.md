# OCR composite proposal and role V11

This defect class is preregistered from aggregate, already-consumed V2 composition evidence. It may not read the V2 fixture archive, V2 scene truths, Chandler, private images, or article images.

P1 is a new Apache-2.0 multi-head model. It keeps the production proposal grouping algorithm and extends the existing visual and geometry tensor with normalized global proposal position, producing `[N,2,32,144]`. Its `[N,10]` output contains two proposal logits followed by eight role logits in the frozen order `YTick`, `XTick`, `AxisTitle`, `PhaseHeading`, `LegendText`, `Participant`, `Annotation`, `Other`.

The split was generated once after preregistration. Its tracked manifests bind 320 training scenes, 96 validation scenes, 128 hidden-public scenes, exact proposal fingerprints, candidate configurations, and runner and evaluator source bundles. P1 is consumed: it passed every fixed validation scene, region, role, and exclusion metric, but its CPU ONNX parity error was `1.0326504707336426e-05`, just above the fixed `1e-05` maximum. P2 is preregistered as a zero-optimizer exact-P1-checkpoint repair that scales all logits by `0.5`, preserving every argmax and probability ordering while targeting only absolute exporter rounding error. P3 remains unregistered. The hidden public archive may be opened once only after selection passes. No passing candidate is production-approved until a separate checksum-bound C# composition gate also proves recognition, roles, markers, provenance, CPU execution, model-store discovery, notices, and packaging.
